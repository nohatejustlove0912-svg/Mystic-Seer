Can Second-Hand Objects Carry Energy?

By Jassy Dagasdas — Mystic Seer · 2026–2027 editorial collection

Objects often carry histories, associations and emotional significance. In esoteric traditions, some practitioners also interpret objects as retaining energetic impressions. That interpretation is part of a spiritual belief system, not an established physical property of second-hand belongings.

When investigating a reported disturbance associated with an object, I would first ask when it was acquired, what changed afterward and whether there are other explanations. A coincidence in timing does not establish that the object caused the experience. Expectations and stories about an object's past may also influence how people perceive it.

Physical inspection matters. An old mirror may have optical distortions; furniture may creak as conditions change; textiles can retain odors. These ordinary characteristics deserve attention before a supernatural explanation is proposed.

For those who choose a cleansing ritual, I frame it as a spiritual practice rather than proof that an attachment existed or was removed. Clean and inspect second-hand items appropriately, respect fire safety and consult relevant specialists when needed. Curiosity and caution can coexist.
