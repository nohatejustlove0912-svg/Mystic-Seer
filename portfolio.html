Intuition, inquiry & discernment

My philosophy

I believe spirituality should deepen awareness, not create fear. My role is not to dictate destiny, but to illuminate possibilities and encourage conscious decisions. Intuition and critical thinking can coexist, while spiritual interpretations and scientific evidence remain distinct. Not everything unexplained is paranormal, and intuitive impressions are not absolute truths.

Investigation methodology

My approach considers witness accounts, folklore research, environmental observation, intuitive impressions, EMF monitoring and dowsing, alongside possible natural explanations. EMF readings and dowsing responses are not conclusive scientific evidence of paranormal activity.
