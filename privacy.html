Privacy Policy

This notice explains how Mystic Seer handles personal information in connection with its services and booking system.

Who is responsible for your information

Jassy Dagasdas, operating as Mystic Seer in the Philippines, is responsible for personal information processed for her independent practice. This notice covers the website, private booking portal, consultations, reports and educational services. Separate Mystic Way activities may have their own privacy arrangements.

Information collected

Depending on your service, information may include your name, email, Messenger name, booking schedule, service selection, payment amount, method and reference, and communications. Readings may require birth date, birth time, birthplace, current city, photographs and questions. Classes may require enrollment details, assignments and assessment records. Please do not send passwords, PINs, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive information.

How information is collected

Information may be provided through email, Messenger, private consultations and the authorized booking portal. The portal verifies your booking code and registered email and records confirmed bookings. Hosting and external platforms may process technical information under their own privacy notices.

Purpose and lawful basis

Information is used to respond to inquiries, verify payments, arrange and deliver services, manage schedules, send confirmations, maintain transaction records and address disputes or legal obligations. Processing is based on applicable lawful grounds under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, including contractual necessity, legal obligations and consent where required. Sensitive personal information is requested only when relevant and processed on an applicable lawful basis, including specific consent where required.

Service providers and disclosure

Google services may be used for booking records, calendar scheduling, email and file storage; GitHub Pages hosts the public website; Meta services may be used for communications. Payment providers process transactions under their own policies. These providers may process data outside the Philippines. Information may also be disclosed where legally required. Public testimonials, identifiable photographs or media appearances require separate permission; booking alone is not permission to publish.

Retention and deletion

Records are retained only as reasonably necessary to provide services, administer bookings, meet applicable accounting and legal requirements, or resolve disputes. Records no longer needed should be securely deleted or anonymized, subject to lawful retention obligations and backup limitations. You may request deletion; requests are assessed against applicable legal requirements.

Security and confidentiality

Access to booking records should be restricted to authorized persons. The booking portal uses a unique booking code and email verification. Keep your code private. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security. Private consultation details should be shared only through agreed private channels.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about processing, access, correction, objection, erasure or blocking, data portability, and withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent. You may also seek damages where legally applicable and lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph. Identity verification may be required before a request is fulfilled.

Children and third-party information

Private client services are intended for adults aged 18 and above. Avoid submitting another person’s personal information without appropriate authority or lawful basis. Do not provide unnecessary identifying details about third parties.

Contact and updates

For privacy requests or concerns, email nohatejustlove0912@gmail.com. This notice may be updated when services or processing practices change. Please review the current notice before submitting information.
