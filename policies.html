Booking Policies & Client Information

Please read and understand these guidelines before requesting a private consultation.

Nature of Psychic & Intuitive Readings

I am a psychic, intuitive reader, tarot and oracle reader, and astrology practitioner — not a fortune teller. My practice involves interpreting intuitive impressions, symbolic messages and what I understand within my spiritual framework as guidance from spirit guides. These interpretations are subjective and do not establish facts or guarantee future events. I have limitations and cannot answer every question or determine a baby's sex.

Professional Fees & Energy Exchange

My purpose is to offer insights and clarity to those seeking guidance. Fees honor the time, preparation, expertise, focus and energy devoted to each service. Payment requirements depend on the selected service and will be communicated before confirmation. Accepted methods: GCash, Maya and bank transfer. A request does not confirm an appointment.

Client Readiness & Free Will

Please ensure you are spiritually, physically, mentally and emotionally ready. Readings may bring up difficult emotions or anxiety; you may pause or decline a reading. Interpretations reflect possibilities, not fixed destiny. Your choices and actions remain your own responsibility.

Psychic, Tarot & Oracle Requirements

For applicable question-based readings, prepare five specific, realistic questions, your full name, birthday, zodiac sign and a recent clear photo. Submit these privately when requested. The five-question requirement does not limit services explicitly advertised with unlimited questions within their session duration.

Weekly Schedule & Availability

Monday, Tuesday & Thursday: Client readings and PDF report processing.

Wednesday & Friday: TV taping; no client readings.

Saturday: Classes and mentorship only.

Sunday: No regular sessions.

All appointments are subject to confirmation.

Astrology & Numerology PDF Reports

Personalized reports take approximately 10–25 working days depending on workload. Reports are processed on Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays only. Only these designated reading days count toward the turnaround time. Detailed analysis requires time and focus. Please do not rush the process and allow for possible delays.

Required details: full name, birthday (month/day/year), exact birth time if known, birthplace and current city. If your birth time is unknown, please indicate this rather than guessing.

Voice Message & Session Expectations

Voice message readings have an estimated 24–48 hour turnaround during designated reading days; non-reading days may extend delivery. Exact start and delivery times may vary with workload and other responsibilities. You do not need to stay awake or wait online; your reading will be sent when ready. You may reply with clarification questions relating to the delivered reading.

Cancellation & Refunds

Confirmed reservations are not eligible for client-initiated cancellation or refunds under the standard booking policy. PDF report payments are non-refundable once the required details have been submitted and payment processed, subject to applicable law. Nothing in this policy limits statutory consumer rights or remedies.

Rescheduling & Attendance

Request rescheduling at least 24 hours before your session. Requests are considered case by case. Late arrival allowance is 10 minutes; please contact me if you expect a delay. Clients must be at least 18 years old.

Medical & Professional Boundaries

I am not a medical doctor. Energy healing is a complementary spiritual practice, not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment. Consult qualified professionals for medical, psychological, legal or financial concerns. Customized services require assessment and quotation before confirmation. No specific outcome is guaranteed.

Privacy & Confidentiality

Personal information is processed to arrange and provide services. Initial inquiries do not confirm bookings. Authorized clients use a separate booking portal that stores registration and booking information. Please review the Privacy Policy before sharing personal information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my appointment confirmed after submitting the form?

No. An initial inquiry is not a confirmed booking. After service arrangements and payment verification, you will receive a unique booking code and private portal instructions. Complete the portal registration to confirm your booking.

How much do customized or on-site services cost?

Fees depend on assessment, location, transportation, preparation, materials and duration.

When will I receive my reading?

Voice message readings: estimated 24–48 hours during designated reading days; non-reading days may extend delivery. Astrology and numerology PDF reports: approximately 10–25 designated reading days (Monday, Tuesday and Thursday), depending on workload.

Can I reschedule?

Request at least 24 hours in advance. Approval is case by case.

Are specific outcomes guaranteed?

No. Services are intended for reflection, personal insight and spiritual exploration.
