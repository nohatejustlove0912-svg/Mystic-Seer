Understanding the Evil Eye Beyond Fear

By Jassy Dagasdas — Mystic Seer · 2026–2027 editorial collection

The evil eye is a belief found in many cultural traditions, often associated with envy, harmful attention or misfortune. In my spiritual practice, protection can be a symbolic way of reinforcing personal boundaries. I do not treat every setback or uncomfortable interaction as evidence of an attack.

When someone feels watched or judged, it helps to distinguish observable behavior from assumptions about another person's intentions. Repeated disrespect, gossip or boundary violations can be addressed directly without claiming to know what someone is thinking or sending energetically.

A protective ritual may hold personal or cultural meaning. It should not become a reason to accuse someone, isolate yourself or spend money out of fear. Practical boundaries, privacy settings and clear communication can be just as important to a person's sense of safety.

My perspective is that spiritual protection should support confidence and discernment, not constant suspicion. You do not have to diminish your achievements to manage someone else's possible envy. Focus on the actions you can control and seek appropriate help when a real safety concern arises.
