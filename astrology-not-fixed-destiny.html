Astrology Is Not a Fixed Destiny

By Jassy Dagasdas — Mystic Seer · 2026–2027 editorial collection

In my astrological practice, a natal chart is a symbolic framework for exploring themes, tendencies and questions about personal development. I do not treat it as a sentence that determines someone's future. Astrology is not scientifically established as a reliable method of predicting events or personality.

Transits are interpreted in relation to the natal chart. An astrologer may associate a period with responsibility, change or reflection, but those themes can manifest in many different ways. Context matters: a person's circumstances, opportunities and decisions cannot be reduced to a planetary placement.

For example, a challenging Saturn transit might prompt a conversation about boundaries, commitments and long-term planning. It does not establish that a relationship will end or a business will fail. A constructive reading asks what practical preparations and choices are available rather than announcing an inevitable outcome.

Use astrological timing as a reflective framework, not as a substitute for financial, medical or legal advice. A chart may inspire questions worth asking; your decisions remain yours. The value I aim to offer is perspective rather than certainty.
